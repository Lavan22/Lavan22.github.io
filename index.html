About

I'm a WSO2 Certified Middleware Developer with over two years of hands-on experience designing, building, and maintaining REST APIs and enterprise integration solutions across banking, government, and education sectors.

My work centers on WSO2 API Manager and Micro Integrator — building proxy services, sequences, and data services that let disconnected systems talk to each other reliably. I handle the full lifecycle: technical design, secure API development with OAuth2/JWT, deployment across Dev–UAT–Production, and the troubleshooting that keeps integrations stable under real-world load.

I care about clean message transformation, sensible throttling and versioning policies, and documentation that the next developer will actually thank me for.
